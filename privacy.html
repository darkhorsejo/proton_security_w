Privacy Policy

App Name: Proton Patrol

Proton Security Services Nigeria Limited

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 1, 2026

Proton Security Services Nigeria Limited ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Proton Patrol mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard user information when our security personnel and guards ("User", "Guard", or "you") utilize the Proton Patrol app on Android devices provided or authorized for security guard tour monitoring.

This App is designed exclusively for enterprise use by authorized security personnel employed by or contracted with Proton Security Services Nigeria Limited. By logging into and using the Proton Patrol App, you acknowledge and agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable Nigerian laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).

PROMINENT DISCLOSURE: Background Location Data Collection

Proton Patrol collects location data—including precise GPS location in the background—even when the app is closed or not in active use.

This background location tracking is a core operational requirement of the Guard Tour System to:

  • Ensure real-time guard tracking and duty verification during patrol shifts.
  • Verify arrival at designated checkpoints, geotagged patrol routes, and client sites.
  • Enable Lone Worker Protection and immediate emergency SOS / panic dispatch for guard safety.

Location collection operates solely during active work shifts or when logged into the Guard Patrol status. Location data is never sold, shared with third-party advertisers, or used for commercial marketing.

1. Information We Collect

To provide effective security tour verification and officer safety services, Proton Patrol collects several categories of personal and operational data:

A. Location Data (Foreground and Background)

  • Precise GPS Location: Real-time latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, and timestamp details collected during active guard tours.
  • Geofencing Data: Automated logs when entering or exiting pre-defined security zones or client facility perimeters.

B. Account & Identity Information

  • Personnel Identifier: Guard ID, full name, employee number, and official phone number.
  • Authentication Credentials: Unique login credentials, PINs, or assigned security tokens.
  • Shift Logs: Clock-in/clock-out timestamps, roster assignments, and duty status updates.

C. Camera and Media Access

  • Checkpoint Scanning: Camera usage for reading QR codes or NFC tags affixed to physical checkpoint locations.
  • Incident & Inspection Photos: Images uploaded by guards to document incidents, property damage, maintenance issues, or patrol proof of presence.

D. Device and Diagnostic Information

  • Hardware Specifications: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and battery level (to monitor guard phone status during night shifts).
  • Network Details: IP address, Wi-Fi connectivity, cellular signal strength, and network provider.
  • App Performance Logs: Error reports, crash logs, and app interaction metadata for maintenance and security troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for security operations, contract compliance, safety assurance, and administrative purposes:

  • Guard Tour Verification: Validating that security patrols are performed in accordance with client schedules and operational protocols.
  • Officer Safety & Emergency Response: Triggering automated panic alerts, man-down detection, and rapid response dispatch if an officer is in distress.
  • Client Reporting & Auditing: Generating verified proof-of-presence reports for corporate and residential clients guarded by Proton Security.
  • Operational Accountability: Investigating security incidents, missed checkpoints, or unauthorized site departures.
  • System Improvement & Security: Maintaining app uptime, troubleshooting software bugs, and preventing unauthorized app access.

3. Disclosure & Sharing of Information

We strictly limit access to your personal and patrol data. Information may only be disclosed under the following circumstances:

  • Internal Command Operations: Authorized control room dispatchers, field supervisors, and management personnel of Proton Security Services Nigeria Limited.
  • Designated Clients: Contracted client representatives receive operational patrol summaries, checkpoint compliance metrics, and incident reports pertaining to their specific premises.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting providers (e.g., encrypted cloud database services) bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Law enforcement authorities or regulatory bodies in Nigeria where mandated by court orders, statutory obligations, or national security considerations.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade guard data or location logs with any third-party advertisers or marketing networks.

4. Data Security

We implement robust physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal and operational data:

  • Data Encryption: All location and incident data transmitted between the app and control server is encrypted using TLS 1.2/1.3 protocols. Data stored on servers is encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Access Controls: Strict role-based access control restricts data viewing to authorized personnel with verified operational needs.
  • Device Pairing: App access is restricted to authorized devices registered within the Proton Security management network.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Patrol logs, incident reports, and location histories are retained in accordance with enterprise record-keeping policies, client contractual requirements, and Nigerian legal statutes:

  • Operational patrol logs and geotags are retained for a default period of up to 24 months for audit and reporting purposes.
  • Account credentials and profile data are retained for the duration of employment or engagement with Proton Security Services Nigeria Limited.
  • Upon employment termination, active access is revoked immediately. Backup data is archived or securely purged according to our corporate data retention schedule.

6. Employee Rights & Data Subject Requests

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), guards and users have rights regarding their personal data, subject to workplace security obligations:

  • Access: The right to request confirmation of personal data held by the Company.
  • Correction: The right to request rectification of inaccurate profile information.
  • Inquiries & Concerns: The right to submit inquiries regarding data handling practices to the Data Protection Officer (DPO).

7. Permissions Requested by the App

To function as intended on the Google Play App Store, Proton Patrol will request the following Android permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: To capture precise GPS coordinates during patrols.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION: To track guard positions continuously during active patrol shifts even when the app is in the background.
  • CAMERA: To scan checkpoint QR codes/barcodes and take incident evidence photos.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To deliver urgent dispatch instructions, shift updates, and safety alerts.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To sync patrol data with control room servers in real time.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect regulatory changes or software enhancements. Any modifications will be updated within the app and on the app's store listing page. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
